# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons using data analytics and statistical techniques.

The analysis focuses on understanding how seasonal conditions influence agricultural productivity, resource usage and economic performance. The project investigates seasonal differences in yield, production, environmental conditions, resource consumption and profitability.

## Objectives

* Explore and understand the agricultural dataset
* Clean and prepare the data for analysis
* Compare agricultural performance across seasons
* Analyze environmental conditions across seasons
* Examine resource usage and efficiency
* Analyze seasonal economic performance
* Investigate crop-level and regional differences
* Identify unusual and unexpected seasonal patterns
* Apply statistical and visualization techniques
* Develop evidence-based insights and recommendations

## Dataset

The dataset contains agricultural information covering:

* Farm and geographical information
* Crop and season
* Farm area
* Rainfall and temperature
* Humidity and sunlight
* Soil conditions
* Nutrient usage
* Irrigation methods
* Fertilizer and pesticide usage
* Yield and production
* Market price
* Cost, revenue and profit
* Water usage and efficiency
* Disease and pest risk

## Key Findings

Kharif demonstrated the strongest overall performance among the three seasons based on the major agricultural and economic indicators analyzed.

The analysis showed:

* Kharif had the highest average yield.
* Kharif recorded the highest total production.
* Kharif generated the highest average revenue.
* Kharif generated the highest average profit.
* Rabi showed intermediate performance across the major indicators.
* Zaid recorded comparatively lower yield and revenue.
* Zaid showed negative average profit, indicating a profitability concern despite generating agricultural revenue.
* Crop-level and regional analysis was used to identify variations from the overall seasonal pattern.
* Environmental and resource-related factors were investigated to understand their relationship with agricultural performance.

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that agricultural performance varies across seasons and that productivity alone does not fully explain economic performance.

Kharif showed the strongest overall performance in the analyzed dataset, while the negative average profit observed in Zaid highlights the importance of considering production costs and profitability alongside yield and revenue.

The findings can support more informed seasonal agricultural planning by considering environmental conditions, resource efficiency, crop characteristics, regional differences and economic outcomes.
